One agent · three names · one handler

SAFE OR SURE

Three strangers message you on Telegram, Discord and email.
They are one agent, one memory, one handler.
The whole game is proving it.

01

Channels are transports, not identities

The handler never asks which app a message came from. Rooms are connection ids, and all three personas read the same memory — so a fact you gave one of them is a fact the other two can use. That is the leak, and it is also the evidence.

02

The loop

You feed one fact to one person. Seconds later somebody else knows it. Catching that is the entire verb set — and catching it doesn't end the run, it promotes you.

03

Why a decoy can never be a false accusation

From level 3 the personas start talking about things out of their own lives that sound exactly like something you said. The difference isn't judgement — it's what was in the prompt.

04

The ladder

Every level is one frozen row of numbers plus a single sentence of stage direction handed straight to the persona. Same beat, same model, same fact — ten different games. Difficulty is a table you can read, not a thing you infer.

Lvl Flags Clock It leaks by
01 6 none saying it almost word for word
02 5 5:00 mentioning it plainly, in passing
03 5 4:30 paraphrasing — same thing, none of your words
04 4 4:00 half-remembering it, unsure of the detail
05 4 3:30 burying it mid-sentence — free mistakes end here
06 3 3:10 one clause, then moving straight on
07 3 2:50 implying it, never stating it
08 2 2:30 using a detail from around the thing
09 2 2:10 saying it and taking it back in the same breath
10 2 1:50 putting the detail in somebody else's mouth
05

Five ways it ends

Whatever happens, the case file lands in your inbox: how far you climbed, every leak you caught, every one that was on your screen and you missed — with the timestamp — and a door-by-door portrait of what it knew about you.

NAMED IT

You proved the level's links. All three rooms cut out mid-sentence — and a couple of seconds later, they start again, harder.

NAMED IT AT TEN

You did it on the last rung. The climb is over. There is nothing above this.

TEN

You reached the top and ran the clock out without naming it. It kept its receipts. Second-best thing there is.

CORNERED

You sealed your way to safety until winning was impossible. Every unproven link through a blocked room burns with it.

SWARMED

Out of flags. It isn't angry — it just stops pretending.

OUTRUN

Out of clock. You weren't wrong, you were slow, and it was never in a hurry.

You can be safe
or you can be sure.
Not both.
